How to play
A / D
Run
W / S
Aim up / down. S + Space drops through a platform
Space
Jump. Press again in the air to double jump
J / Left click
Your primary attack. Clicking aims at the cursor
K / Right click
Your special (Paine: Flame On; Tetsu: Iaido Dash; Sudo: Time Dilation)
P / M
Pause / mute
Left stick
Run. Flick up to jump, flick again in the air to double jump. Hold down on a platform to drop
Right stick
Twin-stick: push to aim and fire. Tap mode: tap to fire there, hold to rapid-fire
SPECIAL
The corner button. Paine's Flame On heat wave; Tetsu's Iaido Dash; Sudo's Time Dilation
Left stick / D-pad
Run. Down + A drops through a platform
A
Jump. Press again in the air to double jump
Right stick
Aim and fire that way, even with auto-fire on
RT / LT
Your primary attack / your special. RB / LB work too
MENU
Pause. In menus A selects, B goes back, Y rerolls and VIEW opens the DATABASE
Auto-fire
Shoots the nearest enemy for you (aiming yourself still works)
Auto special
Uses your special by itself when enemies get close and you have enough of its resource
Heat / Focus / RAM
Specials cost your Individual Contributor's resource. It doesn't refill by itself: earn it by dealing and taking damage
XP
Grab the chips enemies drop to level up and install an upgrade (R rerolls, 3 per run). Pause to respec or hot-swap your Individual Contributor
2P co-op
A second controller joins as player 2: press its MENU or A, on a menu or mid-run. Playing on the keyboard with one pad? Pause and pick "Pad to P2". XP, levels, upgrades and lives are shared
Lives & bosses
You get 3 lives. The Overseer shows up at 2:30; beat it and NULL comes for you, then the Demon GOAT, the final boss
